# Insurance-Data-Analysis

This is a end to end Power Bi project for Prism Insurance Pvt. Ltd. 
Insurance Claims and Policy Analysis Dashboard

# Objective:

To analyze the Insurance Policy and Claims Dataset to uncover customer behavior, claim trends, policy performance, and revenue insights. This analysis aims to help the business improve risk management, optimize policy offerings, identify fraudulent or high-risk claims, and enhance customer targeting strategies.

# Steps Followed
# Questions:
1. What are the most popular policy types and how do they vary across different customer groups?

Analyze policy distribution by:

Age Group

Gender

Active/Inactive Customers

3. What are the number of claims by claim status?  

Identify:

Months with highest claims

Claim frequency trends

Seasonal claim patterns

4. How do customer demographics influence insurance claims?

Compare claims based on:

Age

Gender

Policy Type

Coverage Amount

5. What is the total premium revenue and average claim amount?

Calculate:

Total Premium Amount

Total Claim Amount

Average Claim Value

Coverage-to-Claim Ratios

6. Which claim statuses are most common?

Analyze:

Approved Claims

Rejected Claims

Pending Claims

7. How can these insights support business decision-making?

Improve:

Risk assessment strategies

Customer segmentation

Claim management efficiency

Policy pricing and coverage optimization
